# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers and document-heavy users compressing large, image-rich PDFs in a browser. They need a quick, dependable workflow that keeps private files on their own device and makes the resulting documents easier to share, store, and search.

## Product Purpose

PixelPress converts oversized PDFs into smaller, searchable PDFs entirely in the browser. Auto preserves ordinary documents and flattens only clearly vector-heavy pages, Figma aggressively flattens screens and diagrams, and Custom exposes Auto, Preserve, and Flatten strategies alongside resolution and JPEG quality controls. Selectable-language OCR runs only on pages that lack usable text. Signed files remain byte-for-byte unchanged, and non-OCR results fall back to the source whenever compression would make them larger. Success means meaningfully smaller files without an upload, server dependency, or confusing technical setup.

## Brand Personality

Focused, trustworthy, technically capable. The voice is concise and reassuring, with transparent explanations of browser processing, privacy, quality tradeoffs, and experimental limitations.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy SaaS marketing, decorative dashboards, glassmorphism, neon technical aesthetics, excessive gradients, novelty motion, and interfaces that hide processing limitations behind vague copy.

## Design Principles

1. Keep files local and make that privacy benefit visible.
2. Put the primary action and current processing state ahead of secondary configuration.
3. Explain quality, storage, and OCR tradeoffs in plain language.
4. Show genuine progress and actionable failure states.
5. Preserve a familiar PixelPress workflow while adapting it carefully to browser constraints.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA fundamentals with semantic structure, complete keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, screen-reader status announcements, and reduced-motion support. Maintain full usability at 375px and on large desktop screens.
